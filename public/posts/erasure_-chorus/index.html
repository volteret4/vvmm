Notas Released in a hardback book. Originally released in 1991, the classic Erasure album Chorus was the band’s fifth album and their 3rd UK number one. Release includes B-Sides, Remixes (including 4 new remixes), Rarities, and live “Chorus” performances from the Phantasmagorical Entertainment tour.
